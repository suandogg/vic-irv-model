# Five-seat comparison guide

The testing app includes a Five-seat preference trial comparisons panel below
the statewide primary inputs in Dashboard and Assembly views. Each variant
has a short explanation. Select ON18, ON20, ON24 or the projected baseline
scenario, then the change to compare with reference. These are saved tests with
identical primaries across preference variants, not the interactive inputs.

Pakenham and Yan Yean are currently peri-urban, Morwell provincial, Pascoe Vale
and Ashwood middle ring. The requested selection is useful, but does not include
every model seat class.

## ON18 example: ALP 29, LNP 32, GRN 12, ON 18, IND/OTH 4.5 each

| Seat | Reference final two | Reference winner margin | Matrix-direct winner margin | Source-preserving, no extra layers winner margin |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Pakenham | LNP–ALP | 8.66 | 10.62 | 7.86 |
| Morwell | LNP–ON | 3.62 | 4.53 | 5.27 |
| Pascoe Vale | ALP–GRN | 3.80 | 3.96 | 12.46 |
| Ashwood | LNP–ALP | 0.56 | 1.26 | 0.81 |
| Yan Yean | LNP–ALP | 1.31 | 2.01 | 0.65 |

Margins are percentage points above 50, not winner-minus-runner gaps. Winners
are unchanged in these five seats for these two variants. Pascoe Vale's
source-preserving variant changes the final pair to ALP–LNP: ON is eliminated
before LNP, then GRN is eliminated. Reference eliminates LNP before ON and
finishes ALP–GRN. Thus the larger winning margin is not comparable as a swing.
Its forced ALP–LNP 2PP moves from 63.20% to 62.46%, a fall of 0.74 points.

The panel also shows forced ALP–LNP 2PP and its change, whether the elimination
order changed, and both elimination orders. Morwell's LNP–ON winning margin
and forced ALP–LNP 2PP measure different contests. Compare like with like.

All available variants and scenarios include these same five seats. The panel
comparison choice is separate from the sidebar's active model trial. Further
tests should preserve this five-seat panel and explain flow versus count-path
effects. No accuracy ranking follows from these changes alone.
