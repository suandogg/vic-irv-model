Change: use the synthetic matrix directly in generic ON-related calls while
retaining geography, siphon, floors and caps. Exact federal blending remains
and special priors stay locked. Non-ON calls and all primaries are unchanged.
This separates source selection from the previous removal of extra transforms.

Statewide ALP/LNP/GRN/ON totals move from 38/42/5/3 to 35/45/5/3 at ON18,
31/44/5/8 to 26/47/5/10 at ON20, and 26/38/5/19 to 23/40/5/20 at ON24.

Interpretation: replacing source selection alone reduces ALP forced 2PP in all
five ON18 examples. Removing extra transforms alone had mixed effects. Thus
the matrix-direct result cannot be explained solely as removing duplicated ON
support. It removes other relationships encoded in posterior/prior selection.
This remains a diagnostic bound, not an accuracy-tested production change.
